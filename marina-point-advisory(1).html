Our story

Founded on the Marina, built for the whole country.

Marina Point Advisory opened its first office on Lagos Marina in 2016 with three advisors and one mandate: help Nigerian-owned businesses professionalise without losing what made them work in the first place.

That mandate hasn't changed. We've since opened desks in Abuja and Port Harcourt, added infrastructure finance and market-entry practices, and advised on transactions spanning toll roads, mid-cap manufacturing, and diaspora-backed retail launches — but every engagement still starts the same way: two weeks in your business before we recommend anything.

"The firms that last in Nigeria aren't the ones with the cleverest structure. They're the ones whose structure survives contact with the regulator."— Founding Partner's Note, 2016
